# Comprehensive Post-Market Analysis: Glucashield (2021–2025)
**Project Title:** Strategic Evaluation of Sales Stagnation and Patient Retention  
**Prepared for:** Portfolio Analysis Repository  
**Author:** Yash Patel, Healthcare Data Professional  

---

## 1. Executive Summary
This report details a 5-year longitudinal study of **Glucashield**, an SGLT-2 inhibitor. Despite initial market entry success, the brand experienced a period of stagnant revenue growth between 2021 and 2025. This analysis was initiated to identify the root causes of this plateau and to develop a data-driven pivot strategy focusing on high-value demographics and regional optimization.

---

## 2. Phase I: Performance Diagnostic (Baseline Health)
**Objective:** To quantify the magnitude of the revenue decline and identify the timeline of peak performance vs. current state.

### Methodology
* **Time-Series Analysis:** Tracking Revenue and Units Sold across 60 months.
* **Conditional Benchmarking:** Utilizing green/red threshold indicators to isolate the best (2021) and worst (2025) performing periods.

### Findings
* **Peak vs. Trough:** Revenue peaked at **$11.08M** in 2021 but fell to a 5-year low of **$10.92M** by 2025.
* **Volume Paradox:** Total units sold reached **14.98M**, yet the YoY growth rate turned negative in multiple quarters. This suggests that while volume remains high, the *value* extracted per patient is diminishing.

---

## 3. Phase II: Root Cause Analysis (Market & Pricing)
**Objective:** To determine if the decline is a result of internal pricing strategies or external competitive pressure.

### Methodology
* **Competitive Matrix:** Comparing Glucashield against leaders like Ozempic, Mounjaro, and Jardiance.
* **Unit Economics:** Analyzing "Revenue per Unit" and "Market Share Percentage."

### Findings
* **Pricing Gap:** Glucashield’s unit cost sits at **$3.67**, significantly lower than the category average. This "low-cost" positioning has created a volume trap where high sales do not translate to high margins.
* **Market Stagnancy:** The SGLT-2 category shows a trend of "mild decline/stagnancy." Glucashield holds a respectable **15.4% market share**, but is struggling to capture new territory from entrenched competitors like Jardiance.
* **Cost Sensitivity:** Unlike premium competitors (Ozempic), Glucashield is highly dependent on high-volume transactions to maintain its 3rd-place market position.

---

## 4. Phase III: Demographic & Behavioral Deep Dive
**Objective:** To identify the "Ideal Patient Profile" and locate geographic pockets of untapped or high-performance potential.

### Methodology
* **Segmentation:** Analyzing sales by Gender, Age (10-year brackets), and Province.
* **Retention Modeling:** Calculating the **Churn Metric** (New vs. Lost Patients).

### Findings
* **The "Golden" Demographic:** Analysis confirms a perfect bell curve for the target population, peaking at **Age 50–70**. Gender showed zero correlation, indicating the drug is equally preferred by all genders in this age bracket.
* **Geographic Variance:** * **High Penetration:** St. John's (48%) and Charlottetown (47.8%) are the strongest performing regions relative to their target population size.
    * **Underperformance:** Cities like Barrie (Ontario) show a lower penetration rate (~43%), representing a significant growth opportunity for sales force re-allocation.
* **The Churn Crisis:** The Churn Metric is currently at **~66%**. While the brand acquired ~26,000 new patients, it lost nearly the same amount. This "leaky bucket" is the primary driver of the long-term revenue plateau.

---

## 5. Strategic Recommendations & Action Plan

### **A. Retention-First Strategy (Immediate)**
* **Action:** Launch a "Patient Adherence Program" specifically for the 50–70 age group to reduce the 66% churn.
* **Goal:** A 5% reduction in churn is projected to yield more revenue than a 15% increase in new patient acquisition due to the low cost of retention vs. acquisition.

### **B. Geographic Optimization**
* **Action:** Re-deploy sales representatives from high-competition urban centers in Ontario to Atlantic Canada (St. John's/Charlottetown) where the brand has established trust and higher penetration rates.
* **Goal:** Maximize ROI by focusing on regions with a proven receptivity to Glucashield.

### **C. Payer & PBM Focus**
* **Action:** Since **$28M** of category revenue is driven by **Private Insurance**, the market access team should prioritize formulary negotiations with private payers to ensure Glucashield remains the "preferred" low-cost SGLT-2 option.

### **D. Pricing Adjustment (Long-term)**
* **Action:** Evaluate a marginal increase in unit cost or a shift toward "Value-Based Pricing" to decouple revenue growth from pure volume dependency.
